Didja Spin 107 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Didja Spin 107 ("the app") is a study tool that helps drone pilots prepare for the FAA Part 107 knowledge test. This policy explains what information the app does — and does not — handle.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

How the app works

The app runs entirely on your device and works offline. Any progress or preferences are kept only in your device's local storage and are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes that local data.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience study aid. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
